# Evidence — App Store Metadata

**Name:** Evidence

**Subtitle:** Keep Proof of Your Wins

**Primary category:** Lifestyle

**Promotional text:** Save real compliments, wins, screenshots and feedback. When you need perspective, bring receipts—not generic affirmations.

**Description:**
Evidence is a private, local vault for the things that actually happened: compliments, wins, milestones, feedback, screenshots, and moments you handled well. When you need perspective, Evidence resurfaces your own history instead of generating generic motivational text.

Your entries stay on your device. Optional device authentication can lock the vault. Search lets you find old receipts quickly, and favorites help important evidence resurface more often.

Free includes up to 20 text entries and the core Evidence Mode. The one-time lifetime unlock adds screenshot attachments and premium vault features. No subscription.

Evidence is a reflection and journaling utility. It does not diagnose, treat, or claim clinical mental-health outcomes.
