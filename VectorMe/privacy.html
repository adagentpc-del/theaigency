Vector Me Privacy Notice

Source artwork processing: Vector Me performs raster analysis and vectorization in your browser. Your original PNG, JPG, or WEBP is not uploaded to the Vector Me server by the standard conversion workflow.

Saved projects: If you create an account and choose Save Project, Vector Me stores the generated SVG vector output, project name, selected production use case, conversion mode, and Print Check results. The original uploaded raster image is not included in the saved project.

Accounts: We store your email address, a one-way password hash, export-credit balance, and account creation date. Passwords are not stored in plaintext.

Payments: Payments are processed by Stripe. Vector Me stores purchase identifiers and credit amounts needed to prevent duplicate credit grants and maintain your balance. Payment-card details are handled by Stripe, not by this application.

Analytics: Vector Me records product events such as upload completion, vectorization success or failure, selected use case, checkout starts, downloads, and project actions. The analytics API is designed not to receive the contents of your artwork.

Deletion: You may delete saved projects from My Vectors. Project deletion removes the saved vector record from normal application access.

Security: Saved projects require authenticated ownership checks. Generated SVG is validated before storage and active SVG content such as scripts, embedded images, foreign objects, iframes, and event-handler attributes is rejected.

This notice should be reviewed with counsel and updated with the final operating entity, contact information, retention period, jurisdiction-specific disclosures, and any third-party production services before public launch.
