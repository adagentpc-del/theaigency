# Kill The Loop — App Store Metadata

**Name:** Kill The Loop

**Subtitle:** Close One Thing at a Time

**Primary category:** Productivity

**Promotional text:** Stop staring at giant task lists. Capture what's taking up mental space, see one loop, then do it, schedule it, or deliberately kill it.

**Description:**
Kill The Loop is a deliberately minimal productivity app for unresolved things. Brain-dump what's taking up space, then see one open loop at a time. Do it, schedule it, or consciously decide not to do it. Only then does the next one appear.

There are no projects, kanban boards, priority matrices, or giant default task lists. Scheduled loops return when they're due, with privacy-safe local notifications that don't expose the task text on your lock screen.

Free supports up to 10 active loops. The one-time lifetime unlock removes the limit and supports premium history and future features. No subscription. No account. No backend.
