**Primary category:** Lifestyle

**Promotional text:** Stop deciding whether something is “good enough” to keep. Put your stuff head-to-head, choose your favorites, and discover what you actually love.

**Description:**
Keep One turns decluttering into a fast head-to-head game. Add photos of the things you're trying to sort, choose which item wins each matchup, and let the app reveal your strongest favorites and weakest performers. No home inventory. No AI. No account. Your photos and choices stay on your device.

Use it for clothes, shoes, makeup, bags, fragrance, books, décor, collectibles, or any category where everything feels good enough until two things have to compete.

Free includes one battle with up to 8 contenders. The one-time lifetime unlock supports larger battles, saved history, advanced results, sharing, and future premium features. No subscription.
